### What kind of feedback is welcome?

- 🔤 **New words** — a word you'd like added to an existing category
- 📂 **New categories or subcategories** — topics not yet covered
- 🐛 **Bug reports** — something not working correctly
- 🌍 **Translation issues** — wrong pronunciation or spelling
- 💡 **General ideas** — anything that could make the app better for kids
